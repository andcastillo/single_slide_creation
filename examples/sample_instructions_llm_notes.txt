TITLE: "RELACIÓN UNO A MUCHOS (1:N)"

Subtitle: "Un cliente realiza muchos pedidos; cada pedido pertenece a un solo cliente".

The slide explains a one-to-many relationship in the entity relationship
model, for first-semester database students. All slide text is in Spanish.

In the middle of the slide, from left to right on the same horizontal line:
- A rectangle for the entity "CLIENTE".
- A diamond for the relationship "realiza".
- A rectangle for the entity "PEDIDO".
Connect them with plain lines (no arrows): CLIENTE to the diamond, and the
diamond to PEDIDO. Put the cardinality label "1" just above the line next
to CLIENTE, and "N" just above the line next to PEDIDO.

Above CLIENTE, two attribute ovals connected to it with lines:
"id_cliente" (the key attribute, in bold) and "nombre".
Below PEDIDO, three attribute ovals connected to it with lines:
"id_pedido" (the key attribute, in bold), "fecha" and "total".

At the bottom of the slide, a full-width highlighted box with the text:
"Regla práctica: la clave del lado 1 (id_cliente) viaja como clave foránea
a la tabla del lado N (PEDIDO)."
