TITLE: "MONOVALUADO VS. MULTIVALUADO"

The slide will show the difference in notation for the single valued and multivalued attribute in the entity relationship model. 
single valued are represented by ellipses, and multivalued are represented with a double line ellipse, it it like a ellipse within another ellipse. 
Use the following two examples and text: 

**`fecha_nacimiento: 14/03/2004`** — MONOVALUADO: un solo valor por entidad.

**`telefonos: 300-111-2233, 601-444-5566`** — MULTIVALUADO: puede tener varios valores (celular y fijo).
